Quiz Editor

Create, preview, save and export interactive quizzes with five question types.

Multiple-answer, fill-in-the-blanks, and mark-the-words activities use a Check answer button even in immediate mode.

Publish your quiz

Export a self-contained HTML file with the quiz questions, scoring and a link back to Jason Fetty. Upload it to your website or another compatible host.

To embed a hosted quiz in LibGuides, use its published URL in an iframe, subject to your institution's embedding policies.

Embed your quiz

First export your quiz as HTML and upload it to your website or another public host. Paste its published HTTPS address below to generate an iframe for LibGuides and other compatible websites.

A local file path cannot be embedded on a public LibGuide. Your institution must allow iframe embeds, and the hosting site must permit framing.
